Privacy Policy

Effective 20 August 2026

GoProViewer is a free macOS app that browses and previews the media on a GoPro camera over its USB connection, copies files to your Mac, and — if you choose to connect a Google account — uploads photos and videos to your Google Photos library. It runs entirely on your Mac.

The short version

  • The developer collects nothing. There are no analytics, no tracking, no ads, and no developer servers — nothing you do in the app is ever sent to the developer or to any third party.
  • Everything the app handles stays on your Mac, with one exception: files you select and choose to upload go directly from your Mac to Google Photos.
  • The Google Photos connection is upload-only. The app cannot see, change, or delete anything already in your Google Photos library.
  • Your Google sign-in is stored in the macOS Keychain on your Mac and never leaves it.

Data the app handles on your Mac

All of the following lives only on your computer, under your control:

  • Your media — files copied from the camera are written to the destination folder you chose (by default ~/Movies/GoPro).
  • Thumbnails and media details — cached in ~/Library/Caches/GoProOffload/ so the grid loads quickly.
  • Settings — your preferences (destination folder, filters, and so on), stored in the app's standard macOS preferences.
  • A log file — connection and transfer events are written to ~/Library/Logs/GoProOffload.log to help diagnose problems. It is never transmitted anywhere.
  • An upload record — the file names and sizes of items the app has already sent to Google Photos, kept in ~/Library/Application Support/GoProOffload/ so nothing is uploaded twice.

The app talks to your camera directly over the local USB network link. It never deletes anything from the camera — there is deliberately no delete capability in the app at all.

Google user data

Connecting a Google account is optional. If you do, the app asks Google for two permissions:

  • Add to your Google Photos library (photoslibrary.appendonly) — this permission can only add new items. It cannot list, view, edit, share, or delete anything in your library, including items the app itself uploaded.
  • Basic profile (email address) — used solely to show which account is connected in the app's settings.

What is sent to Google: only the photos and videos you explicitly select and ask the app to upload, together with their file names. Media files can contain metadata recorded by the camera (such as capture time and, if the camera's GPS was on, location); that metadata is part of the file and travels with it, exactly as if you had uploaded the file yourself. Uploads go directly from your Mac to Google over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) — never through any other server. The app never uploads anything automatically or in the background.

What is stored, and where: the sign-in token Google issues is stored in the macOS Keychain on your Mac. The connected account's email address and the record of what has been uploaded are stored locally as described above. None of this ever leaves your Mac.

What is never done with Google user data: it is not shared with, sold to, or transferred to anyone; it is not used for advertising; it is not used to develop, improve, or train machine-learning or AI models; and no human — including the developer — can read it, because it never leaves your computer.

GoProViewer's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data and revoking access

  • Disconnect Google — sign out in the app's settings. This deletes the sign-in token from your Keychain and forgets the account.
  • Revoke the app's access — at any time, from your Google Account's third-party access page.
  • Items already uploaded — live in your Google Photos library, under your Google Account and Google's privacy policy. Manage or delete them in Google Photos; the app has no ability to touch them.
  • Local data — delete the folders and files listed above whenever you like, and remove the app by moving it to the Trash. The app keeps no data anywhere else.

Children's privacy

GoProViewer is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly handle any personal information about anyone — of any age — beyond what is described on this page.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour changes in a way that affects this policy, this page will be updated and the effective date above revised. Because the app collects nothing, meaningful changes are unlikely.
